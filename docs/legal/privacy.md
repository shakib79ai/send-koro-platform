# Privacy Policy

_Last updated November 10, 2026 · Draft for legal review_

What we collect, why, who sees it, and your choices.

## 1. Who this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how [Send Koro legal entity name] (“Send Koro”) collects, uses and shares personal information when you use our websites, apps and services as a **customer**, a **restaurant partner** (owners, managers and staff) or a **courier** using the Send Koro driver app. We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. Information we collect

| Who | What we collect |
|---|---|
| Everyone | Name, mobile number, email; sign-in codes and login history; device, browser and app information; IP address; messages with support; your settings and choices. |
| Customers | Delivery addresses and notes; approximate or precise location if you allow it (to suggest addresses and nearby restaurants); orders, ratings and reviews; Send Koro credit, referrals and promo codes; payment details are collected and stored by Stripe — we receive only the card brand, last 4 digits, expiry and a token. |
| Restaurant partners | Business details (legal name, DBA, entity type, EIN), owner and team member details, locations, food permits and sales tax IDs, menus and photos, hours, order activity, plan and billing information. Bank and identity details are collected by Stripe for payouts. |
| Couriers | Identity and contact details; vehicle type; precise location while you are online and on a trip; trip, pickup and delivery events; drop-off photos; ratings; earnings and tips; tax information collected by Stripe for 1099 forms. Bank and identity verification are handled by Stripe. |

## 3. How we use information

- To provide the marketplace: sign you in, show restaurants, take and route orders, arrange delivery, and show order status.
- To process payments, payouts, plans, refunds and Send Koro credit, through Stripe.
- To keep the Services safe: verify phone numbers, prevent fraud and promotion abuse, and enforce our rules.
- To provide support and send service messages (order updates, receipts, sign-in codes).
- To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations (for example 1099 forms for couriers and records restaurants need for sales tax).
- To improve the Services using aggregated or de-identified data.
- To send marketing only with your consent where required. You can opt out anytime.

## 4. Who we share information with

| Recipient | What they receive and why |
|---|---|
| Restaurants | For each order: your first name and last initial, the items, notes, and for delivery orders the delivery area and courier status. Restaurants may use this only to fulfil your order. |
| Couriers and delivery partners | Pickup details, your first name, delivery address and instructions, and a way to contact you through masked calling or messaging, only to complete the delivery. |
| Customers | For couriers: your first name, vehicle type and live location during the delivery. |
| Stripe | Payment, payout, identity and tax information needed to process payments and meet financial regulations. |
| Service providers | Hosting, text messaging, email, maps and address lookup, customer support and analytics providers who work on our behalf under contract. |
| Legal and safety | Law enforcement or others when required by law, or to protect the safety of any person or prevent fraud. |
| Business transfers | A buyer or successor if Send Koro is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you. |

## 5. Location

Customers: we use location only if you allow it, to suggest your address and show nearby restaurants. Couriers: the driver app collects precise location while you are online and during trips, to send offers, navigate and show customers delivery progress. Location collection stops when you go offline. You can change location permissions in your device settings.

## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our websites use cookies that are needed to sign you in and keep your cart, and optional analytics cookies that help us improve the site. Change your choice anytime in Cookie settings at the bottom of the website.

## 7. How long we keep information

- Account information: while your account is open, then deleted or de-identified within 90 days of closing, except as below.
- Order, payment and tax records: as long as required by tax and financial law (generally 7 years).
- Courier location history: up to 1 year, then deleted or de-identified.
- Drop-off photos: 90 days, unless needed for a dispute.
- Information needed for safety, fraud prevention or legal claims: as long as needed for that purpose.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- See and update your profile, addresses and notification settings in the app.
- **Download your data** or **delete your account** in Account → Privacy center, or email privacy@sendkoro.com.
- Turn off marketing emails and texts in notification settings, or reply STOP to texts.
- Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information, and to appeal a decision about your request. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. We verify requests with a code sent to your phone or email.

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring to protect information. Card and bank details are handled by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your phone and email secure and tell us about any suspected misuse.

## 10. Children

The Services are not for children. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if we learn we have, we delete it.

## 11. Changes

We will post updates here and tell you in the app or by email before material changes take effect.
