# Community Guidelines and Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated November 10, 2026 · Draft for legal review_

How everyone on Send Koro treats each other, and what isn’t allowed.

## 1. Our standards

Send Koro works because customers, restaurant teams and couriers respect each other. These guidelines apply to everyone using Send Koro and to every interaction: in the app, on the phone, at the restaurant and at the door.

## 2. Everyone

- Treat people with respect. No harassment, threats, violence, hate speech or sexual misconduct.
- No discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other protected characteristic. Don’t refuse or rate service based on these.
- Be honest: no fake accounts, false claims, impersonation or misleading information.
- Use contact details shared for an order only for that order.
- Follow the law, including traffic, food safety and health laws.

## 3. Customers

- Give an accurate address and delivery instructions, and be reachable during delivery.
- Report problems honestly. Repeated false refund claims are not allowed.
- One account per person; don’t create accounts to get extra referral rewards or promo credit.
- Tip as you choose; never ask couriers to break traffic laws or rules.

## 4. Restaurants

- Keep your menu, prices, allergens and hours accurate, and prepare food safely in a licensed kitchen.
- Keep permits and sales tax registration current.
- Accept or decline orders promptly; don’t accept orders you can’t make.
- Hand orders only to the courier with the matching order number. Treat couriers well, including access to a restroom where possible.
- Use customer information only to fulfil orders; don’t contact customers for marketing without their consent.

## 5. Couriers

- Drive or ride safely and legally; never use a phone while driving except hands-free.
- Keep food sealed and handle it hygienically; never open or tamper with an order.
- Deliver to the right person or place and follow the customer’s drop-off choice (photo at the door or PIN).
- Never share your account or let someone else deliver for you.
- Don’t ask customers for cash, extra tips or to cancel outside the app.

## 6. Platform misuse

- No fraud, chargeback abuse or stolen payment methods.
- No abuse of promo codes, referrals or Send Koro credit (including multiple accounts, fake referrals or reselling credit).
- No scraping, bots, reverse engineering, or attempts to access accounts or systems without permission.
- No selling or delivering alcohol, tobacco, cannabis, weapons or illegal items through Send Koro.
- No spam or unsolicited messages to other users.

## 7. What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may warn you, limit features, remove content, cancel credit, or suspend or deactivate your account. For serious safety issues we may act immediately and contact authorities. Restaurants and couriers can ask for a review of a deactivation by contacting support within 30 days; we will explain the reason and consider any information you provide.

## 8. Report a problem

Use Help in the app, or **safety@sendkoro.com**. In an emergency, call 911 first.
