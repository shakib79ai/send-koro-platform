# Refund and Cancellation Policy

_Last updated November 10, 2026 · Draft for legal review_

Cancellations, refunds and who pays for what.

## 1. Summary

- You are charged only when the restaurant accepts your order.
- Cancel for free until the restaurant accepts.
- Report a problem within 48 hours in Help, with a photo when you can.
- Approved refunds go back to your original payment method in 5–10 business days. Send Koro credit you used is returned to your wallet.

## 2. Cancelling an order

| When you cancel | What happens |
|---|---|
| Before the restaurant accepts | Free. The hold on your card is released and you pay nothing. |
| After the restaurant accepts, before it’s ready | The restaurant has started cooking. A refund is at the restaurant’s and Send Koro’s discretion and may be partial. |
| After the order is ready or picked up | Usually not refundable, unless there was a problem we can confirm (see below). |
| Restaurant declines or doesn’t answer in 5 minutes | Automatic. You are never charged. |

## 3. Problems we refund

| Problem | Usual outcome (after review) |
|---|---|
| Missing item | Refund for the missing item and its tax. |
| Wrong item | Refund for the wrong item; sometimes a full refund. |
| Order never arrived | Full refund, including delivery fee and tip. |
| Order arrived badly damaged or spilled | Refund for the affected items, or a full refund. |
| Food safety concern | Full refund. Please also tell the restaurant and, if you are unwell, seek medical advice. |
| Very late order | Case by case, often as Send Koro credit. |

## 4. How refunds are paid

- Refunds go to the payment method you used. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
- If you used Send Koro credit, that part is returned to your wallet with a new 30-day expiry.
- Tips are refunded when the order was not delivered. Otherwise tips stay with the courier.
- Taxes are refunded together with the items they were charged on.

## 5. Limits and fair use

Refunds are for genuine problems. We may decline refunds, limit future refunds, or close accounts when we see repeated or false claims, or when a problem was caused by an incorrect address, an unreachable customer or a cancelled order after preparation.

## 6. For restaurants: who pays for a refund

| Part of the order | Paid back by |
|---|---|
| Food, sales tax and restaurant service or packaging charges | The restaurant, from its Stripe balance |
| Customer’s delivery fee | Send Koro |
| Restaurant’s delivery share | Restored to the restaurant when the delivery is refunded |
| Courier tip (only if not delivered) | Send Koro |
| Card processing fee on the original charge | Kept by Stripe. The delivery’s share of that fee is credited to the restaurant on its next plan invoice |

## 7. Contact

Order problems: Help in the app or **help@sendkoro.com**.
