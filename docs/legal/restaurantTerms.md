# Restaurant Partner Agreement

_Last updated November 10, 2026 · SHL Global Consulting Inc., doing business as Send Koro_

Terms for restaurants selling on Send Koro.

## 1. Parties and scope

This Restaurant Partner Agreement (“Agreement”) is between SHL Global Consulting Inc., doing business as Send Koro (“Send Koro”) and the business that signs up (“Restaurant”, “you”). It covers listing your restaurant on Send Koro, receiving orders, delivery, payments and your monthly plan, for every location you add. The [Privacy Policy](privacy.md), [Community Guidelines](usage.md), [Refund and Cancellation Policy](refunds.md) and [Text Message Terms](sms.md) are part of this Agreement.

## 2. What Send Koro provides

- A storefront for each location on the Send Koro website, PWA and apps.
- The restaurant portal and PWA: live orders, menu editor, promotions, hours, team access, earnings and the Stripe tab.
- Delivery arranged by independent couriers (Send Koro drivers or third-party delivery partners), and pickup.
- Customer support for order problems, and menu setup help.

## 4. Payments and payouts (Stripe Connect)

- You must connect a Stripe account and accept the **Stripe Connected Account Agreement**. Customer payments for your orders are made **directly to your Stripe account**; you are the seller of the food.
- Stripe’s processing fee is charged on each payment by Stripe. The delivery money pays its own share of that fee, so you bear processing only on your part of the order (food, tax and your charges).
- For each order, Send Koro receives an application fee equal to the delivery money (the customer’s delivery fee, your delivery share and the tip) minus the delivery’s share of processing. It is used to pay for delivery and is not a commission on food.
- Stripe pays out to your bank on your payout schedule. Send Koro never holds your food money.

## 5. Delivery fees and your delivery share

- You choose a delivery rule for each location: customer pays, you pay all, split (fixed amount or percent), or free above an order amount, with an optional per-order cap and monthly budget.
- Your delivery share for an order is kept from that order’s payment. Changes apply to new orders.
- Delivery areas are set by Send Koro.

## 6. Your responsibilities

- Hold and keep current all licenses and permits needed to prepare and sell food at each location, and maintain food safety standards.
- Keep menu items, prices, descriptions, dietary labels and allergen information accurate. Prices you set are your own.
- **Sales tax:** you set your tax rate and are solely responsible for collecting, reporting and paying sales tax. Send Koro does not collect, file or pay sales tax for you.
- Accept or decline orders within 5 minutes; prepare accepted orders on time and package them securely.
- Hand orders only to the courier with the matching order number.
- Comply with the Community Guidelines and applicable laws, including labor and health laws.

## 7. Refunds, disputes and chargebacks

Refunds follow the [Refund and Cancellation Policy](refunds.md). You request refunds through “Report a problem”; Send Koro issues them. You pay for refunds of food, tax and your charges; Send Koro pays for the delivery part. Card disputes on food are handled in your Stripe account; Send Koro provides order evidence on request.

## 8. Promotions and Send Koro credit

- Restaurant promotions are funded 100% by you and lower the food price; Send Koro charges nothing to run them.
- Send Koro promo credit used by customers on your orders is funded by Send Koro. The card charge on the order is lower by the credit; Send Koro pays the credit back on your next monthly invoice as a negative line, and sends any amount above your plan to your Stripe account in the same monthly run.

## 9. Customer data

You receive customer information only to fulfil orders. You must not use it to market to customers, sell it or share it, except as required by law or with the customer’s consent, and must protect it with reasonable security.

## 10. Content and brand license

You grant Send Koro a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use your name, logos, menu and photos to list and promote your restaurant on Send Koro. You confirm you have the rights to the content you upload. Send Koro may edit content for formatting and accuracy.

## 11. Term and termination

This Agreement continues until ended. You may close a location or your account with 30 days’ notice in the portal; the plan for the current month remains due. Send Koro may suspend a location for safety, legal or payment reasons (an unpaid plan deactivates locations automatically as described under Fees), end this Agreement with 30 days’ notice, or end it immediately for serious breach, fraud or a plan that remains unpaid for 60 days. Amounts owed before the end remain due.

## 12. Liability and indemnity

Each party is responsible for its own acts. You indemnify Send Koro for claims arising from the food you prepare and sell, your taxes, and your breach of this Agreement. Send Koro indemnifies you for claims that Send Koro’s own platform, as provided by Send Koro, infringes a third party’s intellectual property. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or lost profits. Except for indemnities, your payment obligations and a party’s gross negligence or wilful misconduct, each party’s total liability is limited to the plan fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. The Services are provided “as is”, and Send Koro does not guarantee any volume of orders.

## 13. Disputes

Each party will first try to resolve a dispute in good faith by written notice and discussion for 30 days. If unresolved, the dispute will be settled by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, held in Brooklyn, New York or by video, before one arbitrator. Claims are brought only individually, not as a class or representative action. Either party may bring a qualifying claim in small claims court, or ask a court for an injunction to protect its intellectual property or confidential information.
