# Text Message Terms

_Last updated November 10, 2026 · Draft for legal review_

Sign-in codes, order updates and how to stop texts.

## 1. What we text

- One-time sign-in codes (Send Koro uses text codes instead of passwords).
- Order and delivery updates; for restaurants, new-order alerts; for couriers, trip and account messages.
- Marketing messages only if you opt in.

## 2. Consent

By entering your mobile number and requesting a code, you agree to receive these texts from Send Koro, sent by an automated system. Consent to marketing texts is not required to order, sell or deliver on Send Koro.

## 3. Frequency, cost and help

- Message frequency varies with your activity.
- Message and data rates may apply.
- Reply **HELP** for help or contact help@sendkoro.com.
- Reply **STOP** to stop marketing and non-essential texts. You can’t receive sign-in codes after replying STOP to all messages; reply START to turn them back on.
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## 4. Privacy

Your number is used as described in the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md). We don’t sell your number or share it for others’ marketing.
