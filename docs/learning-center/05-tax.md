# Sales tax setup

_Get started · Lesson 5 of 19 · Video 0:26_

You set the rate, collect it with each order, and file it.

## How it works

- Each location has its own rate. We suggest one from the address (NYC: 8.875%); you confirm it.
- Tax is charged on the food price after your own discounts, and on service charges (in NY a service charge is taxable).
- The tax goes to your Stripe account together with the food money. You report and pay it to the state.
- Send Koro never collects, files or pays sales tax.

## Example

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Food after discounts | $30.00 |
| Sales tax 8.875% | $2.66 |
| You receive with the food | $32.66 |

## Good to know

- A location with no rate charges no tax. The item editor warns you when a location has no rate.
- A mandatory gratuity is untaxed only if it’s listed separately, labelled “gratuity”, and paid 100% to staff.
- Your monthly statements in the Stripe tab help you file.
