# Subscription plans

_Money · Lesson 17 of 19 · Video 0:28_

A flat plan for each location, from its last month’s food sales.

## The plans

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,500 | $50 |
| $2,500.01 – $5,000 | $100 |
| $5,000.01 – $7,500 | $150 |
| $7,500.01 – $10,000 | $250 |
| $10,000.01 – $15,000 | $350 |
| Over $15,000 | $500 |

## How it’s worked out

- **Net food sales** = food after your discounts, minus food refunds, for the previous calendar month (New York time).
- Tips, tax, delivery fees and service charges are not counted. Send Koro promo credit doesn’t lower your food sales.
- Each location has its own plan. A new location’s first month is free; a location that isn’t live isn’t billed.
- A month with zero or negative sales pays the lowest plan.
