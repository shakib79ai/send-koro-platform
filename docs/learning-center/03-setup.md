# Store setup

_Get started · Lesson 3 of 19 · Video 0:50_

Profile, photos, hours, prep time, team and alerts.

## Steps

1. **Store profile**: name, cover photo (wide, real food), up to 3 cuisines (the first is your main one), a short description, and extra charges if you use them (service charge or packaging fee, always shown to customers before they pay).
2. **Hours**: weekly hours for each location, plus closures for holidays. Customers can’t order while you’re closed.
3. **Prep time**: your usual time to get an order ready. You can change it for each order when you accept.
4. **Team**: invite people by phone. Owners see everything, managers run orders, menu and hours, staff see live orders.
5. **Notifications**: order sounds that repeat until you answer, plus texts and emails.

## Good to know

- Delivery areas are set by Send Koro for each neighborhood, so there’s nothing to draw. You control Open/Close, hours, prep time and “Accepting orders”.
- Busy? Use **Close store** or slow down prep time instead of letting orders time out.
