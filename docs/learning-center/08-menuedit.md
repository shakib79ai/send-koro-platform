# Edit your menu

_Menu and prices · Lesson 8 of 19 · Video 0:46_

Sold out, per-location changes, bulk edits and option groups.

## Steps

1. **Sold out**: use the switch on the item. Choose “today” (back automatically tomorrow) or “until I turn it back on”.
2. **Bulk edit**: tick items, then Sold out, Available, Move to…, Change price or Delete.
3. **One location only**: choose the location at the top of the Menu page, then change price or availability.
4. **Option groups**: edit a group once on the Option groups tab and every item using it updates. Mark a single choice out of stock (for example oat milk).
5. **Categories**: rename, reorder, hide or choose which menus they appear in.

## Good to know

- Changes show to customers right away.
- Deleting an item doesn’t change past orders.
- Running a deal? Use Promotions instead of lowering the price.
