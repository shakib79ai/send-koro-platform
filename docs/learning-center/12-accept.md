# Accepting an order

_Orders and delivery · Lesson 12 of 19 · Video 0:34_

Accept within 5 minutes; the card is charged only when you accept.

## Steps

1. Read the items, choices and special instructions.
2. Set the prep time (your default is filled in).
3. Tap **Accept**. The customer’s card is charged now.
4. Or tap **Decline** and choose a reason (too busy, item sold out, closing soon). The customer’s hold is released and they pay nothing.
5. When it’s packed, tap **Ready**.

## Good to know

- At checkout the customer’s card is only authorized (held). It’s charged when you accept.
- Orders not answered in 5 minutes are declined automatically. Too many missed orders can pause your store.
- If an item is sold out, mark it on the menu so new customers can’t order it.
