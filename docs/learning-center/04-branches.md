# Locations (branches)

_Get started · Lesson 4 of 19 · Video 0:29_

Run several locations with their own hours, prices, tax and plan.

## Steps

1. Open **Locations** and tap **Add location**.
2. Enter the address, phone and cuisines. The sales tax rate is suggested from the address.
3. Finish that location’s checklist in **Store setup**: permit, sales tax ID, hours, delivery fee rule and payouts.
4. Choose whether its money goes to your main Stripe account or the location’s own Stripe account.

## Good to know

- A new location’s first month is free, and a location that isn’t live yet isn’t billed.
- All locations are billed on one invoice, with one line each.
- On the Menu page, pick a location to change prices or mark items sold out at just that location.
