# Handing the order to the courier

_Orders and delivery · Lesson 13 of 19 · Video 0:27_

Check the order number, hand it over, done.

## Steps

1. Tap **Ready** when the order is packed. The courier is told it’s ready.
2. When the courier arrives, ask for the **order number** (for example SK1052) and check it matches the bag.
3. Hand the order over. The order moves to “On the way” and you’re done.
4. Pickup orders: the customer shows the order number and their name.

## Good to know

- Couriers may be Send Koro drivers or partner couriers. Treat them all the same.
- Couriers deliver with a photo at the door or a 4-digit PIN the customer gives them, depending on what the customer chose.
- Courier late or a problem at handoff? Use **Help** on the order. Please give couriers access to a restroom when asked.
