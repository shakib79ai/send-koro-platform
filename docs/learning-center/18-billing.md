# Paying your subscription

_Money · Lesson 18 of 19 · Video 0:30_

One invoice on the 1st, card or bank, and what happens if a payment fails.

## Steps

1. Open **Subscription** and add a billing card or US bank account (stored securely by Stripe).
2. On the 1st at 12:00 PM ET, your invoice is created with one line per location and charged automatically.
3. See past invoices and download receipts on the same page.

## Example invoice

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Park Slope plan (October sales $6,240) | $150.00 |
| Williamsburg (first month free) | $0.00 |
| Send Koro promo credit paid back | −$15.00 |
| Total charged | $135.00 |

## Good to know

- There’s no proration: plans change on the 1st.
- If a payment fails, Stripe retries up to 4 times over 2 weeks and emails you. Please update your card quickly to avoid interruption.
- Promo credit and some refund fee credits appear as negative lines that lower your invoice.
