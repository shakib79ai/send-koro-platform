# Connect Stripe

_Get started · Lesson 6 of 19 · Video 0:24_

Where your money goes, and how to set it up in minutes.

## Steps

1. Open **Store setup → Payouts** (or the **Stripe** tab) and tap **Connect Stripe**.
2. Stripe’s secure page asks for your business details, the owner’s ID and your bank account.
3. Come back to Send Koro. When Stripe says **Active**, you can take orders.
4. Optional: give a location its own Stripe account instead of the main one.

## Good to know

- You get the full Stripe Dashboard. Stripe asks you to sign in to Stripe for sensitive changes like your bank.
- Refunds are done from the Send Koro order screen, not in Stripe, so the delivery part is refunded correctly.
- You can answer card disputes on your own payments in Stripe.
- Only the owner can open the Stripe tab.
