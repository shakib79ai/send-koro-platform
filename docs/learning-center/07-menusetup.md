# Set up your menu

_Menu and prices · Lesson 7 of 19 · Video 0:27_

Templates, categories, items, sizes and photos.

## Steps

1. Open **Menu → Templates** and pick your type of restaurant (coffee shop, Indian, Bangladeshi, Mexican, burgers, fried chicken, steakhouse and more). Sample items are marked “check price”.
2. Or tap **Import menu** to send photos, a PDF or a CSV. Our team builds it free within 48 hours and you approve it.
3. Add or edit items: photo (wide, at least 1200 × 800), name, description, price or **price by size**, category, labels, dietary tags, spice level and allergens.
4. Add **option groups**: required or optional, how many to pick, free choices, extra prices, and follow-up questions (for example “Make it a meal” → choose a drink).
5. Use **Preview** to see the item exactly as customers will.

## Good to know

- Option groups are shared: build “Milk” or “Spice level” once and add it to many items.
- An item can appear in more than one category.
- Menus and hours let you run breakfast, lunch specials or late-night menus.
