# Food pricing

_Menu and prices · Lesson 9 of 19 · Video 0:39_

Set your own prices with no commission to cover.

## How pricing works

- **One price** or **price by size** (for example 12 oz $4.75, 16 oz $5.25). Customers see “From $4.75”.
- **Choice prices** are added to the item: oat milk +$0.75, steak +$2.50. Free choices make the cheapest picks free.
- **Pickup price** is optional, if you want pickup to cost less.
- **Per-location price** for a location with different costs.
- **Promotions** (BOGO, % off, spend $X save $Y, free delivery) are paid by you and cost nothing to run.

## Good to know

- Prices are what customers pay before tax. Tax is added at checkout at each location’s rate.
- Send Koro adds no service fee, small-order fee or surge fee to your customers.
