# How to get started

_Get started · Lesson 1 of 19 · Video 0:43_

From sign-up to your first order in about a day.

## What you need

- A mobile phone for sign-in codes and order alerts.
- Your food service permit for each location (in NYC, the Health Department permit).
- Your federal EIN and your New York sales tax ID (Certificate of Authority).
- A bank account and the owner’s ID, which Stripe checks when you connect payouts.
- Your menu with prices. Photos help but can be added later.

## Steps

1. Tap **Create account**, enter your name, mobile and email, and verify the 6-digit code.
2. Add your restaurant name, address and up to 3 cuisines, then accept the partner agreement.
3. Open **Store setup** and complete each item: business details, licenses, sales tax, menu, hours, delivery fee rule and payouts (Stripe).
4. When every item is green, Send Koro reviews your food permit and turns your store live.

## Good to know

- Your first month is free. After that each location pays a flat plan based on its last month’s food sales.
- Send Koro takes **$0 commission**. You pay only card processing on your part of each order and any delivery help you choose to give.
- Owners, managers and staff each sign in with their own phone. Add them under **Team**.
