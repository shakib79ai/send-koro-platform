# Referral and promo credit

_Money · Lesson 19 of 19 · Video 0:44_

Customers use Send Koro credit; we pay you back in full.

## How it works

- Customers earn credit from “Refer a friend: give $5, get $5” and Send Koro promo codes. Credit is valid 30 days and covers food and tax only.
- The customer’s card is charged the order total minus the credit, so you receive the credit amount less at the time of the order.
- Send Koro pays it back on your next monthly invoice as a “Send Koro promo credit” line.
- If the credit is more than that month’s plan, the rest is sent to your Stripe account in the same monthly run.

## Examples

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Park Slope plan | $150.00 |
| Promo credit used in October | −$15.00 |
| Invoice | $135.00 |

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Plan | $50.00 |
| Promo credit used | −$80.00 |
| Invoice | $0.00 |
| Sent to your Stripe account | $30.00 |

## Good to know

- Credit doesn’t lower your net food sales, so it never changes your plan.
- Each order shows the credit and the amount paid back later, under **Earnings → Order money**.
- The “Promo credit this month” card on the Subscription page lists every order.
