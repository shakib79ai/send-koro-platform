# Delivery fee: who pays and how

_Orders and delivery · Lesson 10 of 19 · Video 0:51_

Choose your rule: customer pays, you pay all, split, or free above an amount.

## Your options

1. **Customer pays** the full delivery fee.
2. **You pay all** (free delivery for customers).
3. **Split**: you cover a fixed amount (for example $3) or a percent of the fee.
4. **Free above an amount** (for example orders over $30), with optional help below it.

## Extra controls

- A per-order cap: you never pay more than a set amount on one order.
- A monthly budget: once it’s used up, customers pay the full fee.

## Example

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Food | $30.00 |
| Delivery fee | $8.48 |
| Your share (split $3) | −$3.00 |
| Customer pays for delivery | $5.48 |
| Tax 8.875% | $2.66 |
| Tip (100% to courier) | $5.40 |
| Customer card total | $43.54 |

## Good to know

- Customers see one line, “Delivery fee”, and then your discount (“Brooklyn Bowl Co. pays part”).
- Delivery areas are set by Send Koro for each neighborhood.
- Change your rule anytime on the **Delivery fee** page. Each change applies to new orders.
