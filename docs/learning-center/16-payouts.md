# Getting paid (payouts)

_Money · Lesson 16 of 19 · Video 0:26_

How order money reaches your bank.

## How it works

- Each order is a payment on your own Stripe account, created when you accept.
- Stripe pays your bank on your payout schedule. New US accounts usually start with daily payouts a couple of business days after each payment; you can switch to weekly or monthly in Stripe.
- The first payout can take longer while Stripe finishes checks.

## Example

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Food | $30.00 |
| Sales tax | $2.66 |
| Your delivery share | −$3.00 |
| Your part of card processing | −$1.18 |
| Commission | $0.00 |
| You receive | $28.48 |

## Good to know

- Your monthly plan is billed separately; it isn’t taken from payouts.
- Send Koro promo credit used on your orders is paid back on your invoice (see “Referral and promo credit”).
- Instant payouts are off at launch.
