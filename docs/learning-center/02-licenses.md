# Licenses you need

_Get started · Lesson 2 of 19 · Video 0:26_

Only the minimum we need to list you, and what Stripe checks.

## What Send Koro asks for

1. **Business details** (whole business): legal name, doing-business-as name, entity type, federal EIN and the owner or authorized person.
2. **Food permit for each location**: permit number, expiry date and a photo or PDF. In New York City this is the Health Department (DOHMH) Food Service Establishment permit. This is the one document we review.
3. **Sales tax ID for each location**: in New York, the Certificate of Authority number from the Department of Taxation and Finance. Just the number.
4. **Your confirmations**: other licenses your state requires (for example a food protection certificate holder on site), food safety, and the partner agreement.

## Good to know

- There is no VAT in the US. The closest thing is your sales tax ID.
- Stripe asks for the owner’s ID and bank details when you connect payouts. Send Koro never sees your full bank number.
- When a permit is close to expiring, we remind you to upload the new one. An expired permit pauses that location.
- Open **Licenses and legal** to see the exact list for each location’s city or state.
