# Managing orders

_Orders and delivery · Lesson 11 of 19 · Video 0:24_

The live orders board, alerts, and busy-time controls.

## The board

- **New**: waiting for you to accept. Each has a 5-minute timer.
- **Preparing**: accepted, with the time it should be ready.
- **Ready**: waiting for the courier or the pickup customer.
- **Order history**: search past orders by number or customer.

## Alerts

- Order sounds repeat every 10 seconds until you answer.
- Install the PWA on a tablet or phone and keep it plugged in; the screen stays awake on the live board.
- Text and email alerts are in **Notifications**.
