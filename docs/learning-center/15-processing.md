# Card processing fees

_Money · Lesson 15 of 19 · Video 0:28_

What you pay on each order, with a worked example.

## How it works

- Stripe takes its fee (2.9% + 30¢ standard pricing) from the whole card charge, in your Stripe account.
- The delivery money pays its own share of that fee, so you pay processing only on your part: food, tax and service charges.
- Customers are never charged a card processing fee. Send Koro takes no commission.

## Example

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Card charge | $43.54 |
| Stripe fee (2.9% + 30¢) | $1.57 |
| Delivery money’s share | −$0.39 |
| Your part of the fee | $1.18 |

## Good to know

- International cards can cost more; the delivery share is adjusted to the real fee.
- See fees on each order under **Earnings → Order money**, and monthly in the Stripe tab.
